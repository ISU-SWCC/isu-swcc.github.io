Watershed Flow Model

Print model

    The basin is scaled to fit those squares. One square uses the full 24 by 24 inches. With more than one square, a 1/2 inch border is left around the outside so the squares can lock. True north keeps north at the top of the model. Largest fit turns the model so the basin prints as large as it can.

    UTM uses the zone you choose. Albers is an equal-area projection fit to this basin. CONUS Albers is EPSG:5070. The squares refit in the projection you pick. The elevation heights stay the same.

    1 matches the horizontal scale. Less than 1 is flatter. More than 1 is steeper. Every point is at least 0.5 in thick. Nothing is taller than 18 in.

    PLA is the default indoor plastic. PET is tougher and takes more heat. ASA is the hearty outdoor plastic. Bulk is the cheaper filament. Enterprise is the higher-quality filament.

    Draft is faster and coarser. Standard is the usual finish. Fine is slower and smoother.
