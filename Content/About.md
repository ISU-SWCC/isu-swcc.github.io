# Dedicated to conservation

The Soil and Water Conservation Club is the Iowa State University student chapter of the Soil and Water Conservation Society.

## Purpose

The constitution sets four objectives: to acquaint present and potential members with opportunities in soil and water conservation; to develop leadership and a spirit of fellowship; to foster professional attitudes and continued learning; and to promote student interests in the field as they relate to the Soil and Water Conservation Society.

## Membership

Membership is open to registered Iowa State students in good standing who have an interest in soil and water conservation. A student becomes a member by attending at least three meetings. Members keep that standing by attending two meetings or events each semester.

## What the club makes

The club builds a physical groundwater flow model used as a teaching tool, and it publishes Getting Into Soil & Water for a general audience.
